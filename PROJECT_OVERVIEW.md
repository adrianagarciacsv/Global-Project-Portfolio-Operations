# Project Overview: Global Project Portfolio & Executive Operations System

## 1. Business Challenge

Organizations managing multiple clients, projects, departments, and geographically distributed teams need reliable operational visibility.

Without a centralized framework, leadership may struggle to identify delivery risks, monitor project performance, allocate resources, and make timely decisions.

## 2. Proposed Solution

This project introduces a centralized project portfolio management framework designed to support executive oversight and operational coordination.

The solution combines project tracking, KPI monitoring, risk management, and structured executive reporting.

## 3. My Approach

The framework is organized around five operational priorities:

- Portfolio visibility: Centralize project information, milestones, ownership, and status.
- Performance management: Monitor KPIs and identify projects requiring intervention.
- Risk management: Document risks, assign owners, and track mitigation actions.
- Executive communication: Summarize accomplishments, blockers, and decisions required.
- Operational accountability: Establish clear ownership and follow-through.

## 4. Deliverables

### Executive Dashboard
A consolidated view of project volume, status distribution, completion, and high-priority risks.

### Project Portfolio Tracker
A structured register containing project owners, priorities, target dates, budgets, and upcoming milestones.

### Risk Register
A tool for identifying delivery risks, assigning mitigation responsibilities, and monitoring resolution.

### Weekly Executive Report
A reporting structure that communicates portfolio health, decisions required, blockers, and upcoming priorities.

### KPI Definitions
A reference for performance indicators, measurement methods, targets, and reporting frequency.

## 5. Expected Business Value

The framework is designed to support:

- Improved leadership visibility.
- Earlier identification of delivery risks.
- Stronger cross-functional accountability.
- More consistent project reporting.
- Better-informed resource and prioritization decisions.
- Standardized operational follow-through.
